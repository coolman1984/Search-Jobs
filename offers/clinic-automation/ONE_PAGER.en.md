# WhatsApp booking for clinics, approved by your reception team

**Your patients already message you on WhatsApp. Today someone has to answer every message, check the calendar, confirm and remember to send a reminder. Missed messages become missed appointments.**

## What we set up in two weeks
- Patients book on WhatsApp with simple buttons: service → day → time → name.
- Your reception sees every request on one screen and **approves it with one tap** (or proposes another time).
- The patient gets the confirmation, plus a reminder 24 hours before the visit.
- Medical questions always go to a person: the system never gives medical advice.
- Bookings stay in your clinic's own accounts.
